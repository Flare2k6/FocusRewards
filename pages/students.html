Student tools are in development.

FocusRewards for Students is a future concept for older learners who want to build stronger focus habits, earn progress, and connect screen-free time to meaningful goals. This page is a preview, not a live student product yet.

★

Study focus sessions

Students could start focus timers for homework, reading, projects, or test prep and track progress without turning the experience into punishment.

★

Goal-based stars

Future student goals could connect stars to schoolwork, hobbies, outdoor activity, and healthy routines while still keeping families in the approval loop.

★

Reward planning

Students could preview rewards, plan what they are working toward, and understand how daily caps keep the system fair.

Work in progress: The family app prototype comes first. Student-focused features would be explored later with better tracking, safer accounts, and a stronger approval system.
