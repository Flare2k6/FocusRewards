The Problem

Families need more than screen-time punishment.

Parents want healthier digital habits, but constant blocking can turn into a fight. Kids need a reason to choose better replacement habits, not just another rule.

FocusRewards gives families a shared system: kids can see progress, parents can guide the reward economy, and off-phone time becomes something concrete.

The Solution

The FocusRewards loop

A simple demo flow that shows exactly how focused time turns into parent-approved rewards.

1 Focus minutes

A child starts the Focus Timer and spends time away from scrolling.

2 Earn stars

Stars grow based on the parent-set star rate and daily cap.

3 Shop request

The child requests rewards like a smoothie gift card or movie night.

4 Parent approval

The parent reviews progress, approves the reward, and the balance updates.

Child Experience

Progress kids can feel.

Business Model

A family subscription product with a clear roadmap.

Parents are the paying customer. The subscription gives families access to the reward loop, parent dashboard, Star Shop, settings, and child progress views.

Future upgrades include Firebase, real usage tracking, a secure star ledger, stronger parent sign-in protection, and gift card API partners.

Prototype Demo

Built to show the full reward loop for judging.

The current prototype uses local seeded demo state. Real iOS/Android screen-time APIs, Firebase sync, and production tracking are planned later.
